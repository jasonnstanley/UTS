UTS pathway projects develop a formula for better maths engagement

25 September 2017

The pathway projects, launched by the Mathematics and Science Study Centre (MSSC) at UTS, addressed the urgent need for greater student engagement in mathematics.

Many first-year science, technology, engineering and mathematics students were unable to cope with the transition process and the mathematical demands of tertiary study because of limited engagement with mathematics at school.

Through tutoring initiatives and accompanying research demonstrating improved confidence and performance among first-year STEM students, the project increased student wellbeing and retention. Opportunities for student development in mathematics were subsequently presented as part of the enrolment process.
