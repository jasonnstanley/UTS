FUNDED RESEARCH

Intelligent Tutoring Systems, ITS, provide a unique opportunity to engage learners and curriculum in a collaborative technological approach to student development.

Have tested greater than 20,000 incoming students over a decade with consequences to enrollment, a sound approach to such a system is required.

Curriculum engagement enhanced by a tutoring system composed of numerous computer science elements, straddles the learning space in the mathematical and statistical sciences in the transition space.
